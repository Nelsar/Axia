# Code of conduct

Nelsar.Axia follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
That document is the standard; this page says how it applies here.

## In short

- Criticise code, designs and decisions as hard as they deserve. Do not criticise the person who
  wrote them.
- No harassment, insults, personal attacks, or sexualised language or imagery — in any of the
  languages the project is discussed in.
- Bug reports here often carry details of somebody's directory: names, groups, structure. Treat
  them as theirs. Do not repeat, collect or publish them anywhere else.
- Assume good faith. Many people here write in their second or third language, and a sentence
  that reads as rude is more often a translation than an intent.

## Where it applies

Issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and any other space the project runs, and anywhere
someone is speaking for the project.

## Reporting

Write to **nelsar.soft@gmail.com**. Only the maintainers read it, and who reported something is
not shared without the reporter's consent.

A security vulnerability is not a conduct report: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for how to report
one privately.

## What happens

The maintainers decide, in proportion to what happened: a private word, a public warning, a
temporary ban from the project's spaces, or a permanent one — the ladder the Covenant's
enforcement guidelines describe. Comments, commits, code and issues that break this code may be
edited, hidden or removed, and the person concerned is told why.
